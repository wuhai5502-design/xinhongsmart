WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

From enquiry to a defined configuration

1. Requirements review

We read your application, modules and integration needs, then reply with questions if anything is unclear.

2. Model and configuration advice

You receive one or two suitable models with the specification, optional modules and the trade-offs explained.

3. Quotation, sample, production

Quotation follows the agreed configuration. A sample takes about three days and mass production about 25 days after approval.

What to include in your enquiry

  • The application and operating environment (indoor, outdoor, vehicle mounted, glove use)
  • Required data capture: barcode scanning, fingerprint, face recognition, RFID or NFC, ID card reading, document imaging
  • Interfaces and mounting: serial, Ethernet, GPIO, USB, pogo pin, cradle or bracket
  • Software: Android version, pre-installed applications, SDK or API integration, MDM
  • Target quantity, export market and required delivery date

Not sure which model fits? Start from the product range, the biometric tablet overview, or the OEM / ODM customization options.

CONTACT FAQ

Common questions before the first message

How quickly will I get a reply?

Enquiries received Monday to Saturday between 09:00 and 18:30 (GMT+8) are usually answered within one business day. Requests that need a technical review, such as module integration or certification questions, can take two to three working days.

Can I get a sample before mass production?

Yes. A sample is normally ready in about three days for a standard configuration, and mass production runs about 25 days after the sample is approved. The minimum order quantity is 2 units.

What should I include in the first message?

The application and environment, the modules and interfaces you need, any software or integration requirements, and your target quantity, market and delivery date.
