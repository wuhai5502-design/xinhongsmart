OEM & ODM Manufacturing for Rugged Terminals

Shenzhen Xinhong Smart Technology Co., Ltd. manufactures rugged handheld terminals, industrial tablets and biometric devices for system integrators, distributors and brand owners. An OEM programme reconfigures a platform that is already in production; an ODM programme designs the device your deployment needs and takes it through tooling into mass production. Assembly, burn-in, serialisation and inspection run against acceptance criteria agreed with you before the line starts.

This page is the centre for that work. The detail sits in five areas: hardware customization, software customization, industrial design, Android OS customization and certification support.

TWO PROGRAMMES

Start from a shipping platform, or from your requirement

OEM — reconfigure a proven platform

The mainboard and tooling already exist and have been through production, so the engineering risk is low and the route to a sample is short. You choose what changes; everything else stays as validated.

  • Module set, interface layout and memory tier
  • Android build, boot logo and pre-installed applications
  • Housing colour, silk-screen and model name
  • Packaging, manual and accessory set

ODM — design the device around the workflow

When the standard mechanical or electrical design cannot meet the requirement — a different screen size, a housing built into a kiosk or vehicle dock, an extra interface, or a module we have not integrated before — the change is designed, tooled and taken through samples into production.

  • Mechanical and electrical design work
  • New tooling where the housing or mainboard changes
  • Engineering samples and revision rounds
  • Mass production after the sample build passes

The stage-by-stage process is described on the Custom Solutions page — requirements and feasibility, written proposal, engineering sample, verification and certification, mass production, then delivery and support.

QUALITY

Production, quality control and traceability

Inspection runs from incoming materials to finished goods, so the build is checked before it reaches the end of the line rather than after packing. Each unit is serialised, which means a delivered device can be traced back to its production record — useful when a field issue has to be understood quickly rather than argued about.

Before assembly

  • Incoming material inspection against the agreed specification
  • Module set verified against the project bill of materials
  • Firmware image confirmed before units are built

During and after assembly

  • In-process checks during assembly
  • Burn-in before the unit is closed
  • Serialisation and outgoing inspection against the acceptance criteria
ORDERING

Ordering terms

ItemHow it is handled
MOQDepends on how far the configuration departs from the standard platform: a build that reuses existing tooling starts lower than one that needs new tooling or a new mainboard. The confirmed figure is stated in your written quotation.
SamplesA sample of the agreed configuration is built and reviewed against the acceptance criteria before mass production, so the module set, interfaces and firmware are verified on real hardware rather than on a drawing.
Lead timeDriven by tooling scope, sample rounds and the certification work your market requires. You receive a written schedule covering samples, verification and mass production before the first sample is built.
CertificationThe standard build carries GMS certification where the specification lists it, and the capacitive fingerprint module holds Ministry of Public Security certification where used. Requirements your destination market adds — conformity marks, radio approvals, documentation — are agreed before tooling so the test plan fits the schedule instead of extending it.
Documentation and SDKAPI or SDK support is supplied for the modules installed in your build, together with the agreed specification sheet. Tell us at the start if your procurement process requires particular records, so they are included in the project scope.
Payment and shippingCommercial terms, incoterms, carton and pallet specifications and shipping arrangements are agreed with the quotation.

If your project involves designs, drawings or market plans you would rather not share without an agreement in place, say so before sending documents and a mutual NDA can be put in place first.
