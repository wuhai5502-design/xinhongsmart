The team that writes these pages

Articles on this site are signed by the Xinhong Smart Engineering Team. This page says who that is, what they work on, and what they check before a figure is published.

In short: fifteen engineers in Shenzhen across three disciplines — hardware interfaces and modules, mechanical design and tooling, and Android OS and software. The guides are written from acceptance criteria and test results on finished units, not from datasheets alone.

Three disciplines, one build

Hardware and modules. Interfaces, connectors, scan engines, fingerprint sensors, RFID radios, GNSS. The work that decides whether a module fits a housing and whether its SDK behaves the same way on the firmware in production as it did on the bench.

Mechanical design and tooling. Housings, seals, mounts and the tooling for an ODM build. This is also where an ingress or drop rating is lost if a change re-opens a seam, which is why those changes are scheduled rather than slipped in.

Android and software. The OS image, drivers, the SDK and the API surface the integrator builds against. An Android build is a product that keeps moving after shipment: patches, MDM behaviour and the lifetime of the version are part of the specification, not an afterthought.

How a figure gets published

Three rules, and they are the reason some answers on this site are shorter than a buyer expects.

Figures come from the specification sheet. Where the sheet lists a capacity but no runtime, the page says so rather than inventing hours. Where a band list belongs to one market's build, the page names that market and asks for the band table instead of implying worldwide coverage.

Certification is only claimed where a certificate exists. GMS on the models whose sheet lists it, Ministry of Public Safety certification on the capacitive fingerprint module, and the IP rating the model is tested to. Radio approvals are scoped per project and quoted, never assumed.

Test conditions are stated with the number. A 10-hour battery figure is 1080p playback at 70% volume and brightness. A 1.2 m drop is onto a wooden floor with the unit powered on. Read the condition and the number together or the number means nothing.

What sits in the factory

Hardware design, assembly, firmware loading, burn-in, serialisation and final inspection run in our own facility in Shenzhen: 2,000 m², three production lines, capacity of 10,000 units a month. Test equipment includes a high and low temperature chamber, vibration tester, salt spray tester, water ingress bench and abrasion tester, and modules are checked on the finished device rather than on a bench sample.

Every unit is serialised, so a field failure can be traced to its production record, test data and module lot. The About page lists what can be verified independently, including the public business registry record and the option of a factory audit on site or by video before a first order.

Where the writing comes from

Most guides start as a question a buyer asked twice: why a device that survives a drop still fails in a freezer, what an RFP should specify so the acceptance test is enforceable, why a quotation moves when the module set changes. If a question keeps arriving, it usually deserves a page.

That is also why the site carries the negative answers. A 12-inch tablet is the wrong device for one-handed picking; a 10-hour battery figure is not a shift; an IP67 device is not rated for wash-down. Those lines do not help a quotation, and they save a project.

Frequently asked questions

Who writes the articles on this site?
The Xinhong Smart Engineering Team, with the sales engineering team on commercial questions such as minimum order, timing and quotation structure. Articles are signed with the team rather than an individual because the content is reviewed by whoever owns that discipline.
Do you publish test reports?
The site publishes test conditions and figures. The reports themselves come with a quotation when a tender requires one by name, for example a MIL-STD-810H method or a specific radio approval.
Can a customer visit or audit the factory?
Yes. A factory audit can be arranged on site in Shenzhen or by video before a first order, and the public registry record is linked from the About page so the company can be checked independently.
