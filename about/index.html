OUR COMPANY

Connecting devices to everyday operations

Shenzhen Xinhong Smart Technology Co., Ltd. supplies biometric handheld terminals, industrial tablets and handheld PDAs for mobile data collection and identity workflows. Our range also includes A3 document camera scanners, digital signature pads and dual-screen ID verification terminals.

We work with system integrators, distributors and enterprise buyers to discuss device configuration, software integration and deployment requirements. The starting point is the work your team needs to complete: what to capture, how to connect and where the device will be used.

Our OEM and ODM support covers device interfaces, data capture modules, firmware, branding and packaging. Standard features and optional modules vary by model, so the configuration is reviewed for each project.

FACTORY & CAPABILITY

Are we a real manufacturer? Here is what you can check

Overseas buyers usually start with one question — is this a factory or a trading company? Below is the information we put on the table, including the public registry record you can verify yourself before you send an enquiry.

Company name

Shenzhen Xinhong Smart Technology Co., Ltd.

深圳市歆鸿智慧科技有限公司 — a registered manufacturer in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. Brand name: Xinhong Smart. The same legal entity operates this site and our China site.

Factory

Design, assembly and test in our own facility

Hardware design, assembly, firmware loading, burn-in, serialisation and final inspection are carried out in our own 2,000 m² facility in Shenzhen. A factory audit — on site or by video — can be arranged before you place an order.

Production line

One flow for standard and customised builds

Three production lines cover the range. Standard models and customised builds run through the same assembly, burn-in and packing flow. Branding, colour box, printed manual and accessory set are packed to the order, and carton and pallet specifications can be adjusted to the way your market receives goods.

R&D team

Engineering on three levels

Fifteen engineers work on hardware interfaces and modules, mechanical design and tooling, and Android OS and software, with SDK and API support for integration with your systems.

Testing equipment

Ratings checked on the finished unit

In-house test equipment: high and low temperature chamber, vibration tester, salt spray tester, water ingress test bench and abrasion tester. Each build is verified against the ratings published for the model — ingress protection (IP65 or IP67 depending on the model), operating temperature range and battery endurance — and put through burn-in before packing. Scan engine, fingerprint, NFC and ID card modules are tested on the finished device, not on a bench sample.

Quality control

Five inspection gates, serialised units

Incoming material checks, in-process checks during assembly, burn-in, serialisation and outgoing inspection against the acceptance criteria agreed for your project. Because every unit is serialised, a delivered device can be traced back to its production record.

Certifications

What the standard build carries

GMS certification on the models whose specification lists it, Ministry of Public Security certification for the capacitive fingerprint module, and IP65 or IP67 ingress ratings per model. Conformity your market requires is scoped into the project before tooling — see certification support.

Export markets

Built for the destination, shipped from Shenzhen

We ship to the United States, Germany, Singapore, Uzbekistan, Tanzania, Seychelles and Mauritius, among other markets. LTE bands, charger plug types, language and labelling are matched to the destination market, so devices arrive usable with local carriers and sockets. Shipments leave Shenzhen under incoterms agreed with the order.

Years of experience

Registered since 2016 — ten years in the field

Ten years supplying industrial rugged tablets, handheld PDAs, biometric terminals, document scanners, signature pads and counter-top ID terminals to system integrators, distributors and brand owners.

OEM / ODM ability

Both routes, on our own platforms

OEM reconfigures a platform already in production; ODM takes a new device through tooling into mass production. Interfaces, modules, housing, Android image, branding and packaging are all in scope — see the OEM & ODM centre.

Production capacity

Sample in 3 days, mass production in 25

Capacity is 10,000 units per month. MOQ is 2 units, and a sample of the agreed configuration is built in about 3 days and mass production runs about 25 days from sample approval. Larger or repeat volumes are scheduled in writing with the quotation.
