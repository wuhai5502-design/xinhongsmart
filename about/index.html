OUR COMPANY

Connecting devices to everyday operations

Shenzhen Xinhong Smart Technology Co., Ltd. supplies biometric handheld terminals, industrial tablets and handheld PDAs for mobile data collection and identity workflows. Our range also includes A3 document camera scanners, digital signature pads and dual-screen ID verification terminals.

We work with system integrators, distributors and enterprise buyers to discuss device configuration, software integration and deployment requirements. The starting point is the work your team needs to complete: what to capture, how to connect and where the device will be used.

Our OEM and ODM support covers device interfaces, data capture modules, firmware, branding and packaging. Standard features and optional modules vary by model, so the configuration is reviewed for each project.
