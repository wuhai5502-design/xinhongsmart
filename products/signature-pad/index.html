Frequently asked questions

Will the digital signature hold up legally?
The pad captures biometric-stroke data (pressure and dynamics) rather than a flat image, which is materially stronger evidence of genuine signing. Legal validity ultimately depends on your jurisdiction's e-signature framework — we supply the raw stroke and identity data your compliance layer needs.
Can we integrate it with our own signing software?
Yes — the device exposes standard interfaces to your application. Share your integration environment and we will confirm the path and provide documentation.
What does the encryption chip do?
The optional SM2/SM3/SM4 chip signs and encrypts signature data inside the device, protecting the chain between pad and backend — required in some regulated domestic deployments.
Is this the same as signing on a capacitive touchscreen?
No, and the difference matters. Capacitive finger-signing produces a low-fidelity trace; the electromagnetic pen captures 5,080 lines per inch with 2,048 pressure levels — closer to ink on paper.
