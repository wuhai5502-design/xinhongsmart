Frequently asked questions

Which Android and memory configurations are available?
Android 12 and 4 GB RAM with 64 GB storage are standard. Android 13, 6 GB + 128 GB and 8 GB + 128 GB are optional configurations. The TF slot supports storage expansion up to 512 GB.
Are barcode, fingerprint and RFID modules standard?
No. Barcode scanning, capacitive or optical fingerprint capture, ID card recognition and LF/HF/UHF RFID modules are optional. Built-in 13.56 MHz NFC is listed in the standard configuration. Confirm your required modules and software compatibility before ordering.
Does the XH-500 include wired Ethernet?
RJ45 10/100 Mbps Ethernet is optional. The standard interfaces include USB-A, USB Type-C, a 3.5 mm headphone jack, a 3.5 mm DC power input, one SIM slot and one TF slot.
What does the 10-hour battery figure mean?
The listed 10-hour endurance is for 1080 HD video playback at 70% volume and 70% screen brightness. It is not a fixed runtime for every workload; cellular use, optional modules, applications and brightness affect battery life.
Can the cameras and SIM configuration be changed?
The standard cameras are 5 MP front and 13 MP rear, with optional 8 MP front and 20 MP rear cameras. One SIM slot is standard and dual SIM is optional. Confirm the combination required for your order.
