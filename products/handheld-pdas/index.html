How to choose

  • RFID is part of the job — the 5-inch PDA takes a UHF RFID module alongside NFC, so one device covers pallet reads, shelf reads and item-level barcode capture.
  • Multi-shift operation — the XH-200 has a removable 5,100 mAh pack, so swapping a battery keeps the device in service instead of on a charger.
  • Label quality varies — tell us what your codes look like. Honeywell and Zebra engines differ on damaged, low-contrast or poorly printed labels.
  • Integration work — both platforms expose module APIs through the SDK, so output formats should be agreed before the order is placed.

Deployment patterns, mount options and scanner choices are covered in the warehouse deployment guide; module and interface changes are handled through the hardware customization route.

Handheld PDA questions

Can a handheld PDA read UHF RFID tags as well as barcodes?

The 5-inch industrial handheld PDA can be built with a UHF RFID module alongside NFC for ISO 14443A cards at a 1 to 3 cm read range, and it keeps 2D barcode scanning. That combination covers pallet and shelf reads as well as item-level barcode capture in one device. Confirm the tag population and read distance you need before the build is specified.

Is the battery removable?

The XH-200 uses a removable 5,100 mAh Li-polymer pack, so a spare battery keeps a multi-shift operation running without a charging break. The 5-inch handheld carries a built-in 4,000 mAh pack. Both are rated for roughly eight hours of active use.

Which barcode scan engines are available?

The XH-200 is offered with Honeywell or Zebra 2D scan engines, and the 5-inch PDA supports 2D barcode capture as well. Engine choice affects decode performance on damaged or low-contrast codes, so tell us what your labels look like and we will specify the module to match.

What is the minimum order and lead time?

MOQ is 2 units, a sample takes about 3 days and mass production runs about 25 days. Where a configuration needs new tooling or a new mainboard, the schedule for that programme is stated in your written quotation.
