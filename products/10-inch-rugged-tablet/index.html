Frequently asked questions

What does IP67 actually cover?
IP6x means dust-tight — no ingress. The 7 means the unit survives temporary immersion in up to 1 m of water. It does not cover high-pressure jets (that is IP69K); if you need washdown-rated gear, tell us and we will advise.
Can I get it with Windows instead of Android?
Yes — Windows 11 is an optional OS for this housing. Choose it when your MES/WMS requires a Windows client; otherwise Android 12 with MDM gives simpler fleet management.
How does the 6,000 mAh battery last a full shift?
Typical scan-and-sync usage draws little power; ~8 hours of active use is realistic. For double shifts we recommend multi-bay charging cabinets — ask us about docking accessories.
Is the screen readable in direct sunlight?
Yes, the IPS panel is a high-brightness, sunlight-readable unit. If your deployment is permanently outdoors (yard operations), mention it — we can advise on brightness and hood options.
