Frequently asked questions

Which fingerprint module options does the XH-100 offer?
Three options: FAP10 integrated module, FAP20 optical sensor and FAP30 optical sensor. All capture 500 dpi / 256 grey-level images and meet the FBI FIPS-201 image quality criteria (FIPS PIV IQS / ISO 19794-4).
Can it scan QR codes and damaged 1D barcodes?
Yes. The 2D imager covers UPC/EAN, Code 39/128/93, Codabar, ITF, PDF417, Data Matrix, QR Code, Aztec, Han Xin and postal symbologies, with optional OCR (OCR-A/B, E13B MICR).
Is the battery replaceable?
The 5,000 mAh battery is built-in and non-detachable, delivering more than 8 hours of continuous work. For multi-shift operations we recommend charging cradles between shifts.
Can the RAM and storage be upgraded?
Yes. The base build is 2 GB + 16 GB; 4 GB + 64 GB and 6 GB + 64 GB are available as factory options. A Micro SD card up to 128 GB adds local storage.
