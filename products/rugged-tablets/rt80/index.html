RT80 5G 10.1-Inch Rugged Android Tablet

A 10.1-inch IP67 tablet on the UNISOC UIS7885 platform: eight cores to 2.7 GHz with an 8 TOPS NPU, 8 GB + 128 GB, a 1200 × 1920 display and a 3.8 V / 10000 mAh battery. 5G, an independent BeiDou receiver and a 48 MP rear camera are on the standard build.

10.1-inch IPS at 1200 × 1920 and 400 nits, 10-point G+G capacitive touch.
UNISOC UIS7885, octa-core to 2.7 GHz with an 8 TOPS NPU; Android 13, with a HarmonyOS 6.1 build listed.
8 GB + 128 GB, TF card expansion to 512 GB.
5G SA/NSA, WiFi 5 dual-band, Bluetooth 5.0 and 13.56 MHz NFC.
IP67 sealing and a 1.2 m drop rating; operating range −10 to +50 °C.

Where it fits

Store and retail operations: the 10.1-inch screen is large enough for a product list or a queue-ticket application at a counter, and the 48 MP camera reads labels and shelf edges without a separate handheld. The use cases published for this model are store management and retail management, which is a counter-and-aisle job rather than a warehouse one.

Warehouse and stock counts: with the optional 2D imager the tablet handles receiving, put-away and cycle counts; without it, it is a 5G terminal for the applications that only need a screen and a keyboard. Compare it with a handheld PDA if the work is one-handed picking at height — 650 g in one hand for a full shift is a different proposition from 250 g.

Outbound and dispatch: 5G, independent BeiDou and a 10000 mAh battery suit a device that leaves the building. The band set is the constraint to check first, not the hardware.

Frequently asked questions

Is the RT80 5G a global version?
The specification is the China build: 5G N1/N3/N5/N8/N28/N41/N78/N79, LTE B1/B3/B5/B8/B34/B38/B39/B40/B41 and WCDMA B1/B5/B8. For another market the band set and the radio approvals are confirmed before production. Treat the table as the starting point, not as a promise for your carriers.
Does it run Android or HarmonyOS?
Android 13 is the listed build, and a HarmonyOS 6.1 build is also listed for this model. Which one ships depends on your order; confirm the build your application has been validated on before the sample is made, because the two are not interchangeable for a kiosk or MDM deployment.
What is standard and what is optional?
Standard: 5G, 4G and 3G radios, dual-band WiFi 5, Bluetooth 5.0, 13.56 MHz NFC, power-key fingerprint unlock, GNSS with an independent BeiDou receiver, 8 MP front and 48 MP rear cameras, 8 GB + 128 GB. Optional: 2D barcode imager, FAP20 capacitive fingerprint capture, offline ID card reader and the 8 MP rear macro camera.
How long does the 10000 mAh battery last?
The specification lists capacity, not runtime, so any hours figure would be invented. Runtime depends on the radios in use, screen brightness, the application and which optional modules are fitted. If runtime is a selection criterion, ask for a power measurement on a unit configured like your build.
Does it include Google Mobile Services?
GMS is not listed in this specification, which is expected for a China build. If your application depends on Google Play services, raise it before the order so the build and the certification can be scoped together rather than discovered at the pilot.
