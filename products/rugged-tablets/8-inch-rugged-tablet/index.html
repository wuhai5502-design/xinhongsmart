8-Inch Rugged Industrial Tablet

A one-handed-balance Android tablet built for warehouses, inspection routes and vehicle mounts — with the scanning, battery and connectivity options that consumer tablets never get right.

Why teams pick this model

  • All-day battery, no charging panic. 10,000 mAh delivers a full 8-hour shift with over 300 hours standby — enough for double shifts sharing devices between runs.
  • Built for drops, not desks. Rated for 1.5 m drops while powered on, with Corning 3rd-generation tempered glass over the touch panel.
  • Real data capture, not an afterthought. Choose Honeywell 6603, Zebra SE4710 or NLS CM60 2D imagers, plus optional fingerprint (FBI PIV FAP20) and offline ID card reading.
  • Mounts and docks without hacks. A 12-pin Pogo Pin connector powers vehicle docks and charging cradles — no exposed USB ports wearing out.
  • Stays connected off-site. Dual-band WiFi, full-band 4G LTE and BeiDou/GPS/Glonass positioning as standard.

Where it earns its keep

Warehouse and 3PL: cart-mounted picking with a 2D imager, putaway confirmation and cycle counting on one device. Pair it with our 5-inch handheld PDA for one-handed scan-intensive picking.

Field service and inspection: full-band 4G keeps work orders syncing outside the building; the Pogo Pin dock charges in the vehicle between sites.

Manufacturing floor: DC-in and Pogo mounting suit fixed stations and AGV trolleys; the sealed housing shrugs off dust and washdown-adjacent areas.

Frequently asked questions

Is this tablet suitable for one-handed use?
At 890 g it is a two-handed device for extended holding, but balances well on a forearm strap or pick cart. If your workflow is scan-heavy with one hand free, the 5-inch handheld PDA at 242 g is the better fit.
Can it read damaged or screen-displayed barcodes?
Yes, when configured with a 2D imager (Zebra SE4710 or Honeywell 6603). Both decode screen codes and damaged/DPM labels. We recommend testing decode rates on your actual labels before rollout.
Does it support MDM and zero-touch enrolment?
Yes. The Android 12 build supports GMS and standard MDM platforms (kiosk mode, remote provisioning and app push), so fleets can be deployed without touching each unit.
What is the MOQ and lead time?
MOQ is 2 units. A sample takes about 3 days and mass production about 25 days once the configuration is confirmed. Send the configuration you need through the contact form for a written schedule.

Tell us your quantities, target market and required modules (scanner, fingerprint, 4G bands). We reply with configuration advice and pricing — usually within one business day.
