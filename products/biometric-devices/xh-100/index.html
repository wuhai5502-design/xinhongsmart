XH-100 Biometric Handheld Terminal

A 5-inch Android terminal built around identity: NXP NFC, full-symbology 2D scanning and optional fingerprint capture modules in a 400-gram body that runs a full shift on one charge.

Identity-first design — fingerprint (FAP10/20/30), NFC and ID workflows on one device.
Reads everything — 1D, 2D, stacked, postal codes and even OCR (optional).
5000 mAh for >8 h continuous work, >500 h sleep standby.
Dual SIM + full-band LTE for field deployments anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Which fingerprint module options does the XH-100 offer?
The listed options are an FAP10 integrated module, an FAP20 optical sensor and an FAP30 optical sensor. Select one module at order stage. Confirm its model, active area, image dimensions, sampling, SDK formats and any required certification for the selected hardware and software configuration.
Can it scan QR codes and damaged 1D barcodes?
Yes. The 2D imager covers UPC/EAN, Code 39/128/93, Codabar, ITF, PDF417, Data Matrix, QR Code, Aztec, Han Xin and postal symbologies, with optional OCR (OCR-A/B, E13B MICR).
Is the battery replaceable?
The 5,000 mAh battery is built-in and non-detachable, delivering more than 8 hours of continuous work. For multi-shift operations we recommend charging cradles between shifts.
Can the RAM and storage be upgraded?
Yes. The base build is 2 GB + 16 GB; 4 GB + 64 GB and 6 GB + 64 GB are available as factory options. A Micro SD card up to 128 GB adds local storage.
