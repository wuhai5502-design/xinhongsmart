XH-200 6-Inch Android Handheld PDA

A 282-gram Android 12 terminal with a screen big enough to read a full dispatch table and a battery you can swap between shifts — built for warehouses, delivery rounds and retail floors that scan all day.

6-inch-class 1440 × 720 screen with Corning third-generation tempered glass.
Removable 5100 mAh pack — swap instead of waiting on a charger.
Your choice of scan engine: Honeywell HS7, Zebra SE4710 or NLS-CM60.

Where the XH-200 fits

Warehousing and picking: 5.99 inches is enough to show a full pick list without scrolling, and the removable battery means a two-shift operation never waits for a charge cycle.

Retail floors and stock counts: NFC handles contactless cards and loyalty passes at 1–3 cm, while the optional 2D imager reads shelf labels, QR payment codes and damaged printed barcodes.

Delivery and field rounds: full-band LTE plus Beidou/GPS/GLONASS keeps drivers connected and every scan stamped with a location.

Cold-chain and industrial sites: the unit keeps working down to −20 °C and shrugs off a 150 cm drop onto concrete while powered on.

OEM / ODM, factory direct

Scan engine choice, NFC configuration, memory tier, colour, boot logo and pre-installed apps are all decided before the line starts. LTE bands can be matched to your target market on production orders, and packaging, labels and documentation can follow your own brand.

Frequently asked questions

Which barcode scan engines can be built into the XH-200?
Three factory options: Honeywell HS7, Zebra SE4710 and NLS-CM60. All are 2D imagers with 5 mil optical resolution and read PDF417, MicroPDF417, Data Matrix, QR Code, Micro QR, Aztec, MaxiCode and Han Xin, including inverse variants.
Is the battery user-replaceable?
Yes. The 3.7 V / 5100 mAh Li-polymer pack is removable, so a shift can swap a fresh battery instead of parking the device on a charger. Expect roughly 8 hours of active use and more than 300 hours on standby.
Does the XH-200 have an IP sealing rating?
The standard datasheet specifies a 150 cm drop test while powered on, −20 °C to +55 °C operation and 95 % non-condensing humidity. Ingress protection depends on the sealing configuration — tell us your site conditions and we will quote the matching build.
Will it work on European and Asian LTE networks?
The standard radio covers LTE B1/B3/B5/B7/B8/B20/B38/B39/B40/B41 with WCDMA 1/2/5/8 and GSM 2/3/5/8, which covers most European, Asian and African carriers. Bands can be adjusted for other markets on production orders.
