What to confirm before you order

  • Which card paths your market actually uses — magnetic stripe still carries real volume in some markets while contactless dominates in others. The XH-600 covers all three, but confirm which ones your acquirer will enable.
  • Where the receipt goes — the 58 mm printer is inside the unit, so paper loading and roll size are part of the daily routine. Tell us the receipt length and we size the roll guidance to it.
  • Whether cardholder verification needs a fingerprint — the 500 DPI module is optional and is only worth the cost where the flow requires it over a PIN or a signature.
  • Certification scope for your destination — PCI PTS 5.x, EMV L1/L2, Paywave, Paypass, PBOC 3.0 and the UnionPay approvals are listed, but which ones your build ships with depends on the market and the acquirer.
  • Integration format — Android 15 with SDK access means your application drives the card paths and the printer directly. Output formats and the API surface should be agreed before the order is placed.

Module selection, memory, housing, boot logo and packaging are handled through the hardware customization route; firmware and application changes go through Android OS customization.

POS terminal questions

Does the printer need a separate power supply or dock?

No. The 58 mm thermal printer sits inside the XH-600 body and runs off the same 2,800 mAh 7.4 V pack as the rest of the terminal, so a receipt is printed from the unit in your hand. A 3,600 mAh option is available where a shift is long enough to need it.

Which card types can one terminal accept?

All three in one unit: magnetic stripe on tracks 1, 2 and 3 with bi-directional swiping, contact IC cards at 5.0 V, 3.0 V or 1.8 V, and contactless NFC covering ISO/IEC 14443 Type A and B plus Mifare1. The IC and contactless paths are built to EMV and PBOC, and a card reading indicator confirms a successful read.

Is the fingerprint module standard?

It is optional. The module is specified at 500 DPI over a 15.24 x 20.32 mm active area and is listed against STQC and FBI single-finger capture profiles FAP10, FAP20 and FAP30. Add it where the payment flow needs cardholder verification beyond a PIN; leave it out where a signature or PIN is enough.

What certifications does the terminal hold?

PCI PTS 5.x, EMV Contact Terminal L1 and L2, EMV Contactless Terminal L1, Paywave, Paypass, PBOC 3.0 L1 and L2, QUICS, UnionPay Terminal Safety Certification, UnionPay POS Terminal Safety Assessment, plus 3C, FCC and CE. Tell us your destination market and we confirm which approvals your configuration ships with.
