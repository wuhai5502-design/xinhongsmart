XH-600 4G Smart POS Terminal

An Android 15 handheld POS terminal with a 58 mm thermal printer built into the same body as the card readers, so a payment and its receipt come out of one unit. Magnetic stripe, contact IC and contactless NFC are all standard, the fingerprint reader is optional, and the certification list covers PCI PTS 5.x, EMV L1/L2, Paywave, Paypass and PBOC 3.0.

Built-in high-speed 58 mm thermal printer — no separate printer or dock.
Magnetic stripe on tracks 1, 2 and 3 with bi-directional swiping.
Contact IC cards at 5.0 V, 3.0 V or 1.8 V, plus contactless ISO/IEC 14443 Type A and B with Mifare1.
Android 15.0 with 4 GB RAM and 32 GB eMMC, 4G / 3G / 2G, hotspot, Bluetooth, GPS and AGPS.
Optional 500 DPI fingerprint module listed against STQC and FBI FAP10, FAP20 and FAP30.

Where the XH-600 fits

Chain supermarkets and convenience stores: one unit takes the card and prints the receipt, so a lane does not need a terminal, a printer and the cabling between them. The 5.5-inch screen and 202.4 mm body sit in a hand through a full shift.

Restaurants and bakeries: table-side payment and a printed receipt from the same device, with 4G keeping the terminal connected when the venue WiFi is saturated.

Delivery and mobile collection: 4G / 3G / 2G with hotspot plus built-in GPS and AGPS, so the terminal works away from a fixed counter and the transaction carries its location.

Queue-busting and seasonal lanes: dual SIM and dual SAM slots let the terminal carry the acquirer configuration and a fallback, which keeps a temporary lane running when one path is unavailable.

Flows that need cardholder verification: add the optional 500 DPI fingerprint module where the payment requires more than a PIN, or use the touchscreen for signature capture where it does not.

Frequently asked questions

Does the XH-600 print receipts without a separate printer?
Yes. A high-speed 58 mm thermal printer is built into the body and shares the same 2,800 mAh 7.4 V battery as the rest of the terminal, so the receipt is printed from the unit the operator is already holding. A 3,600 mAh pack is available where a shift runs long enough to need it.
Which card types does it accept?
Magnetic stripe on tracks 1, 2 and 3 with bi-directional swiping, contact IC cards at 5.0 V, 3.0 V or 1.8 V, and contactless NFC covering ISO/IEC 14443 Type A and B plus Mifare1. The IC and contactless paths are built to EMV and PBOC, and a card reading indicator confirms a successful read.
Is the fingerprint reader included?
It is optional. The module captures at 500 DPI over a 15.24 x 20.32 mm active area and is listed against STQC and FBI single-finger profiles FAP10, FAP20 and FAP30. Specify it where the payment flow requires cardholder verification beyond a PIN; the terminal also supports signature capture on the touchscreen.
Can it run our own payment application?
The XH-600 runs Android 15.0, so your application drives the card paths, the printer and the scanner directly through the SDK. Encryption for the PIN pad covers DES, 3DES, RSA, SHA-256 and AES. Agree the API surface and output formats before the order is placed.
