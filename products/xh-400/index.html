Where the XH-400 fits

Production lines and fixed workstations: the native RJ45 port keeps data flowing where WiFi is congested, and IP65 sealing handles the dust and wash-down that a production floor produces.

Vehicles and forklifts: 880 g with a 300 × 183 mm body mounts cleanly on a RAM arm, and the 10,000 mAh pack plus DC charging suits long shifts on a vehicle power supply.

Identity and enrolment counters: combine the offline ID card reader with a fingerprint sensor and face-capable camera work flow, and the tablet becomes a single-window registration station that does not depend on network quality.

Warehouse receiving and dispatch: add the rear 2D engine from Newland, Honeywell or Zebra and use the same unit for bin labels, waybills and returns.

Integrators: internal USB, TTL serial, GPIO and SPI headers are exposed inside the housing, so custom peripherals can be wired without external adapters.

OEM / ODM, factory direct

Module selection, memory tier, display resolution, Android version, housing colour, boot logo, pre-installed applications and packaging are all decided before the line starts. We can also match LTE bands and charger plugs to your destination market.

Frequently asked questions

Is the XH-400 really IP65 sealed?
Yes. IP65 is listed in the standard specification: dust tight and protected against low pressure water jets. Because the housing is sealed, the 10,000 mAh polymer pack is charged through the DC port or the USB Type-C port, with a charging indicator on the unit.
Can it connect over wired Ethernet?
Yes, the XH-400 has a native RJ45 port, which suits fixed workstations, production lines and kiosks where WiFi is unreliable. It also carries USB-A, USB Type-C, a DC 3.5 mm charging port and a hybrid SIM/TF tray that takes two of three cards at a time.
Which identity and scanning modules are available?
Optional modules include the ST21NFC NFC reader at 13.56 MHz covering ISO/IEC 14443 Type A/B and ISO/IEC 15693, a second-generation Chinese ID card reader that works offline, FAP10 capacitive or FAP20 and FAP30 optical fingerprint sensors, and rear-mounted 2D engines from Newland, Honeywell and Zebra.
Does it ship with Google Mobile Services?
The standard build runs Android 10.0 or Android 11.0 with GMS certification, so Play Store and Google services work out of the box. A plain AOSP build without GMS is also available on request.
