A full-size 1920 × 1200 screen for forms, drawings and dashboards, powered by a 10,000 mAh pack that holds a shift — with room inside for ID reading, face verification or a 2D scan engine.

Where the XH-300 fits

Field inspection and maintenance: the 1920 × 1200 panel shows a full checklist or schematic without scrolling, and the 10,000 mAh pack covers a working day away from a socket.

Identity counters and registration desks: optional ID card reading plus face verification turns the tablet into a single-counter enrolment station, and both modules run offline so rural sites are not blocked by network quality.

Warehouse and production supervision: add the NLS-N1 2D imager for bin labels and part numbers, and three assignable keys give operators one-handed shortcuts for the actions they repeat most.

Digital signage and kiosks: with dual speakers, Type-C OTG and a TF slot up to 128 GB, it also works as a fixed information or self-service terminal.

Frequently asked questions

Can the XH-300 read national ID cards and verify faces offline?
Yes, as factory options. The ID module is a Ministry of Public Security certified hardware decoder that reads second-generation resident ID cards without a network connection. The face module works at 0.2 to 1.5 metres with claimed verification accuracy of at least 99.72 percent and a response under one second.
Is the 10000 mAh battery removable?
No. The 3.7 V / 10,000 mAh pack is built in and non-removable, which lets us seal the housing better. It delivers roughly 8 hours of active use and more than 300 hours on standby; for shift work we recommend a charging cradle or dock.
What storage and memory options are available?
Standard build is 3 GB RAM with 32 GB storage. Factory options are 4 GB + 64 GB and 6 GB + 128 GB, and a TF card adds up to 128 GB of external storage.
Does the datasheet list an IP rating or drop height?
The published datasheet specifies a six-face rolling test to 1,000 cycles, humidity at 95 % non-condensing and storage from −20 °C to +60 °C. Ingress protection and drop ratings depend on the sealing configuration, so send us your site conditions and we will quote the matching build.
