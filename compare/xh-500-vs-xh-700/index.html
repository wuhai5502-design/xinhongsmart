XH-500 vs XH-700: Two 10-Inch Rugged Tablets

Both are 10.1-inch Android tablets with a 1200 × 1920 panel and a 10000 mAh battery. Almost nothing else is the same: the XH-700 is the newer platform with 5G and an Ubuntu option at 14.6 mm; the XH-500 is the thicker build with physical function keys, an RJ45 option and a lower operating floor.

Quick answer: choose the XH-700 for 5G, Ubuntu, a faster platform with an AI accelerator and a 650 g body; choose the XH-500 for the −20 °C floor, the PTT and F1/F2 keys, wired Ethernet or an LF/HF/UHF RFID module. Both are quoted at a 2-unit minimum.

Both are IP67: dust-tight, and sealed against temporary immersion rather than continuous. IP68, which is rated for continuous immersion at a stated depth, is on the 8-inch XH-800 — the IP rating guide covers where the line falls, and a tender that names a code should ask for the test report by name.

Same screen, different generation

The two tablets read the same on a purchase order — ten-inch Android, 1200 × 1920, 10000 mAh, 2-unit minimum — and then diverge. The XH-500 carries a MediaTek MTK8788 with a Mali-G73 GPU and 4 GB of RAM as standard: an older platform that is still entirely serviceable for scanning, forms and WMS screens. The XH-700 puts a UNISOC UIS7885 with an 8 TOPS NPU and a 16 GB + 256 GB tier in a body that is 5.4 mm thinner and about 450 g lighter.

That weight gap is the number to notice. 1.1 kg against 650 g decides whether a device can be carried on a shoulder strap for a shift, and it is the reason the XH-700 exists despite the XH-500 having a larger module catalogue.

Pick the XH-500 if

The application is a fixed Android 12 image. If the APK, the MDM agent and the driver set have already been validated on Android 12, moving to a newer platform means re-validating them. The XH-500 ships Android 12 as standard, with Android 13 as an option rather than a default.

You need the cold floor or the wired port. The XH-500 is rated to −20 °C, which is 10 degrees below the XH-700, and it carries an optional RJ45 Ethernet port. Vehicle docks, fixed stations and cold rooms are its natural territory.

The keys are part of the workflow. PTT, F1 and F2 are physical buttons on the XH-500. If operators push to talk or run a shortcut from a gloved hand, a touchscreen-only build is a step backwards.

You need LF or HF RFID. The XH-500 offers LF 125/134 kHz, HF 13.56 MHz and UHF as separate optional modules. The XH-700 does not publish an RFID module.

Pick the XH-700 if

5G is a requirement. The XH-700 covers SA/NSA on N1, N3, N5, N8, N28, N41, N78 and N79 with MIMO; the XH-500 is 4G only. That is not a detail to discover at the pilot.

The application needs a Linux build. Ubuntu is available next to Android 13 on the XH-700 and on nothing else in the range.

On-device inference is in scope. The 8 TOPS NPU changes what can run locally — image checks, OCR, lightweight models. The MTK8788 has no comparable accelerator.

It will be carried. 650 g and 14.6 mm against 1.1 kg and 20 mm. For inspection rounds, field service and any job where the tablet is in a hand rather than on a mount, that difference is the whole decision.

What they share

A 10.1-inch 1200 × 1920 panel, 10000 mAh, a 1.2 m drop rating, NFC 13.56 MHz as standard, 2D scanning as an option, a 2-unit minimum with a sample in about three days, and the same ordering route. Both are configured to order rather than picked from stock, and the cost-of-ownership note applies to either.

What this table cannot tell you

It carries no price: both are built to configuration and the module set moves the number more than the model does — the pricing note explains what does. It also does not carry radio approvals, which are scoped per market and quoted separately. And for either model, a tender that names an IP code or a MIL-STD method should ask for the test report by name rather than rely on the datasheet.

Frequently asked questions

Is the XH-700 a replacement for the XH-500?
No. They overlap on screen size and battery and diverge on everything else. The XH-500 keeps the cold rating, the physical function keys, the RJ45 option and the RFID modules; the XH-700 adds 5G, Ubuntu, a faster platform and a much lighter body.
Can the XH-500 run Android 13?
Yes, Android 13 is an option on the XH-500; Android 12 is the standard build. The XH-700 ships Android 13 or Ubuntu.
Which one survives a cold store?
The XH-500, at a stated −20 to +60 °C. The XH-700 is rated −10 to +50 °C. Battery behaviour below zero is the constraint either way — the cold storage notes explain why.
Do both support the same MDM platforms?
Both are Android devices, so the usual Android Enterprise tooling applies, but the OS build differs: Android 12, Android 13 or Ubuntu. Confirm the agent against the exact build rather than the model.
What is the minimum order?
Two units on either model: one for a field trial, one for application and device-management testing. A sample of an agreed configuration is built in about three days and production runs about 25 days from approval.
